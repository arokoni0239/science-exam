About True Clothing BD

Customer-centric sourcing with global standards.

Who we are

A prominent apparel sourcing company based in Bangladesh.

True Clothing BD Ltd. is known for its global reach and commitment to quality. With a team of skilled professionals, the company specializes in providing world-class apparel and fashion products.

We excel in producing high-quality woven, knit, and sweater items, catering to the needs of buyers across Europe, America, South America, and Canada.

“Our core values, mission, and vision are rooted in a customer-centric approach — delivering products that meet the highest standards in both price and quality.”
True Clothing BD Ltd
Our mission

Exceptional experience through quality, service, and value.

Our mission is to provide an exceptional customer experience through superior product quality, excellent service, and unmatched value. We are dedicated to satisfying our customers and ensuring timely delivery.

We aim to be a global leader in the textile and apparel industry, offering high-quality garment manufacturing, innovative products, and outstanding services.

We are committed to building a socially responsible organization that adheres to international standards, while inspiring creativity and joy. Our focus is on delivering compelling and memorable designs with a strong emphasis on sustainability.
