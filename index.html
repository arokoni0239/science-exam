How we work

A clear path from brief to delivery.

Transparent costing, factory matching, sampling, and production oversight — built for international fashion brands.

Step 01

Briefing & Costing

Share your tech pack, quantity, and target price. We clarify details and return clear MOQs and lead times.

Step 02

Sampling & Development

Proto, fit, and pre-production samples managed with feedback loops until specs are locked.

Step 03

Production & QC

Local oversight across fabric, cutting, sewing, and finishing with quality checks before release.

Step 04

Shipping & Support

Export documents, logistics coordination, and shipment visibility through to delivery.
