Our services

Full-service apparel sourcing & production support.

What we deliver

Six pillars that keep your supply chain moving.

From first brief to final shipment, True Clothing BD Ltd operates as an extension of your buying office in Bangladesh.

01

Research & Development

Market research and trend-led development help you shape ranges that balance design ambition with manufacturability and cost.

02

Product Design

Design collaboration that turns concepts and tech packs into clear specifications ready for sampling and bulk production.

03

Production

Factory allocation and production management for knit, woven, sweater, denim, footwear, and accessories programs.

04

Operations Support

Day-to-day follow-up on fabric, cutting, sewing, and finishing — so issues are flagged early, not after shipment.

05

Quality Assurance

Inspection and quality control aligned to buyer standards, protecting brand reputation before goods leave Bangladesh.

06

Logistic Support

Export documentation and shipping coordination with your nominated forwarder or trusted logistics partners.

Process

The apparel sourcing process.

Step 01

Briefing & Costing

Share tech pack, quantity, and target price. We clarify missing details and provide transparent costing, MOQs, and lead times.

Step 02

Factory & Sampling

We match your product to the right production partner, then manage proto, fit, and pre-production samples through approval.

Step 03

Production & QC

Local teams follow the critical path with on-site checks so quality standards are met before any shipment is released.

Step 04

Shipping

We coordinate logistics and documentation so you receive on-time, compliant deliveries with clear visibility.

Compliance

Associate production units with recognized certificates.

We work with production partners that hold industry certificates and memberships covering labor rights, workplace safety, and environmental responsibility.
